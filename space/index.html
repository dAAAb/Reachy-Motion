Reachy Mini app

Reachy-Motion

Talk with Reachy Mini — it moves with what it says, in real time.

  • Three voices — OpenAI GPT-Live-1, your ElevenLabs agent, or Taiwanese (台語) on a local Mac.
  • Speech-driven motion — every clause becomes a gesture that lands with the audio.
  • An embodied agent — switch mode or volume by voice, “look at me”, “what do you see?”, live web answers.

Install it from the Reachy Mini dashboard, then add your API keys on the app's settings page. Keys stay on your device.
